# FirmBenchmark™ Grading Rubric

All grades are benchmarked against the Esquire Interactive managed law firm cohort (50+ active projects in Ahrefs). This is not an industry average — it is the average managed law firm.

## Grade Scale

| Grade | Cohort Position | What it means |
|-------|-----------------|----------------|
| A | Top 10% | Well above cohort median on this dimension |
| B | Top 25% | Above median, approaching market-leading |
| C | Middle 50% | Near cohort median — functional but not competitive |
| D | Bottom 25% | Meaningfully below median — structural underperformance |
| F | Bottom 10% | Critical gap — non-functional on this dimension |

---

## Search Authority (Domain Rating)

| DR | Grade | Notes |
|----|-------|-------|
| 50+ | A | Elite authority |
| 40–49 | B | Strong, above cohort median |
| 30–39 | C | At or near cohort median |
| 20–29 | D | Below median — growing gap |
| 0–19 | F | Critical gap — authority foundation missing |

**Cohort median DR: ~35**

Also factor in: if DR is significantly lower than the firm's primary competitors in their city (>15 point gap), drop one grade.

## AI Visibility (Score out of 25)

| Score | Grade |
|-------|-------|
| 18–25 | A — Strong, being recommended consistently |
| 13–17 | B — Solid presence on most platforms |
| 8–12 | C — Partial visibility, gaps present |
| 4–7 | D — Minimal, not competitive |
| 0–3 | F — Critical gap, effectively invisible |

**Cohort median: ~10/25**

## Local & Entity Signals

Estimate from: directory presence in web search results, Knowledge Panel status, NAP mentions.

| Observation | Grade |
|-------------|-------|
| KP present + 6+ directories + strong review velocity | A |
| KP likely + 4–5 directories + decent reviews | B |
| No KP confirmed, 2–3 directories, average reviews | C |
| No KP, 1–2 directories, weak reviews | D |
| Not findable in local search, missing entirely | F |

| Ahrefs health score | Grade |
|--------------------|-------|
| 90–100 | A |
| 80–89 | B |
| 65–79 | C |
| 50–64 | D |
| <50 | F |

**Cohort median: ~84/100**

---

## Overall Grade

Average the five dimension grades (A=4, B=3, C=2, D=1, F=0). Convert back:
- 3.5–4.0 → A
- 2.5–3.4 → B
- 1.5–2.4 → C
- 0.5–1.4 → D
- <0.5 → F

**Modifier rules:**
- If AI Visibility is F, drop the overall grade by one step
- If DR is below 20 for a firm 10+ years old, note this as unusual and flag it explicitly
- If the firm ranks below #3 for their own branded name, that's a severe signal — flag in the report

## EI Cohort Context Line

Always include this line in the report:

> All grades reflect competitive position against Esquire Interactive's active client cohort of 50+ managed law firm projects tracked in Ahrefs. This is a proprietary comparison set — not an industry average. A firm in the middle of this cohort is performing at the average managed law firm, not the average law firm website.
